Metabolic stages

Understand your fasting stages

As your fast progresses, your body moves through distinct metabolic states. HonestFast tracks your current stage in real time so you always know exactly where you are in your fast.

From the initial fed state all the way to deep autophagy, see your current stage update as you fast - and understand what your body is doing at every step.

1

Fed State

Body digesting and absorbing nutrients from the last meal

0-4 hrs
2

Catabolic State

Blood sugar normalizes, insulin drops, glycogen begins depleting

4-8 hrs
3

Fat Burning

Glycogen stores depleted, body switches to burning fat for fuel

8-12 hrs
4

Ketosis

Liver produces ketone bodies; brain and body run on ketones

12-18 hrs
5

Autophagy

Cellular cleanup begins - damaged cells are broken down and recycled

18-24 hrs
6

Deep Autophagy

Peak cellular renewal and immune function regeneration

24+ hrs
Islamic fasting

Built for Ramadan

HonestFast includes a dedicated Ramadan mode with tools designed specifically for fasting during Ramadan - from automatic Sehri and Iftar times to prayer times and Qada tracking.

☾️

Automatic Ramadan detection

HonestFast uses the Hijri calendar to automatically detect Ramadan. No manual setup needed - the app knows when Ramadan begins and switches to Ramadan mode.

🌏

Sehri and Iftar times

Astronomically calculated Sehri (Suhoor) and Iftar times for your exact location. Set individual reminders so you never miss Sehri or Iftar.

📌

Prayer times

Full prayer times for your location - Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Supports both location-based and city selection for accurate times worldwide.

📋

Qada fasting tracker

Track missed Ramadan fasts and your Qada (makeup) fasting. Keep a clear record of which fasts still need to be made up throughout the year.

🅾

Sunnah and voluntary fasts

Plan and track voluntary Islamic fasts alongside your regular routine. Track Ramadan, Qada, and voluntary Sunnah fasts in one place throughout the year.

🕑

Voluntary Fajr to Maghrib

Log voluntary Sunnah fasts from Fajr to Maghrib. HonestFast calculates the fast duration automatically based on your local prayer times.
