🌙 Islamic Fasting

Built-in Ramadan mode with prayer times

Honest Fast automatically detects Ramadan and calculates precise Sehri and Iftar times based on your location using astronomical methods.

🕌

Auto-Detected Ramadan

The app automatically recognizes Ramadan using the Islamic (Hijri) calendar and switches to special Ramadan mode with enhanced features and reminders.

Astronomically calculated using the Muslim World League method. Get precise prayer times (Fajr through Isha) for your location with automatic timezone handling.
