How to set up medication reminders and track doses on iPhone

Missed doses are easiest to prevent with a simple, repeatable routine. This guide shows how to list each medicine, set the times you take it, log each dose, and keep track of refills, so you always know what is due next.

Jump to the steps

Step 1: List every medicine

Write down each medicine you take, including the name, the strength and the form (tablet, capsule, liquid). Add any supplements you take regularly. A complete list is the base for every reminder that follows.

Step 2: Set the dose times

For each medicine, set the times of day it is due, such as morning, midday, evening and bedtime. Match these to the instructions on your prescription or label. If a medicine should be taken with food or at a set interval, note that in the schedule as well.

Step 3: Turn on reminders

Allow notifications for the app, so a reminder appears at each dose time. Keep the reminder sound and alert settings on, and check that Do Not Disturb does not silence them during your usual dose times.

Step 4: Log every dose

When you take a dose, mark it as taken, and mark it as skipped if you do not take it. A dose log turns a memory question ("did I take it this morning?") into a record you can check, and it shows patterns over time, such as a dose that is often missed in the evening.

Step 5: Track refills before you run out

Note how many doses or days each supply lasts, and set a refill reminder a few days before you run low. Ask your pharmacy how long a refill takes so you can order in time.

Step 6: Review the record with your doctor or pharmacist

Bring your dose log and medicine list to appointments. A clear record of what you took, and what you missed, helps your doctor or pharmacist decide whether a schedule or dose needs to change. Do not change a dose yourself based on the log alone.

Medication reminder checklist

  • Every medicine and supplement listed with its strength and form
  • Dose times set to match your prescription or label
  • Notifications allowed, and Do Not Disturb checked
  • Each dose marked as taken or skipped
  • Refill reminders set a few days before each supply runs out
  • Dose log shared with your doctor or pharmacist at checkups
This is not medical advice. Follow the instructions from your doctor or pharmacist. If you miss a dose or are unsure what to do, ask your pharmacist or doctor before taking more.
