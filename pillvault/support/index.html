Frequently asked questions

How do I add a medication?+

Tap the plus (+) icon on the main screen, then enter the medication's name, form (tablet, capsule, liquid, etc.), dosage, and schedule. You can add your prescribing doctor's details, personal notes, and start/end dates for short-term courses. Save, and it appears in your medication list ready to log.

What dose schedules does PillVault support?+

PillVault supports daily, weekly, interval, and custom schedules — and you can add multiple dose times throughout the day. It also supports tablets, capsules, liquids, drops, sprays, powders, creams, patches, injections, inhalers, and more.

How do dose reminders work?+

When you add or edit a medication, set the times you take each dose. PillVault schedules personalised reminders for each dose time. You can also see your next dose on the Lock Screen via Live Activities, and use Siri & Shortcuts to log doses or check your adherence hands-free.

How does refill tracking work?+

Enable supply tracking for a medication and enter your current quantity. PillVault automatically estimates your remaining days of supply based on your dosing schedule, shows color-coded refill status indicators, and sends a reminder before your supply runs low so you never run out unexpectedly.

I marked a dose by mistake. Can I undo it?+

Yes. After marking a dose as taken, you can tap undo to reverse it instantly — keeping your dose history and adherence statistics accurate.

What is medication adherence and how does PillVault track it?+

Medication adherence is how consistently you take your prescribed medications on schedule. PillVault tracks daily, weekly, and monthly adherence, shows completed and missed doses, builds streak counters, and provides a calendar-based history so you can see patterns at a glance. Premium unlocks advanced analytics and detailed reports.

How do medication reports work?+

PillVault can generate a detailed medication report covering your 30-day dose history, adherence analysis, calendar-based patterns, refill status, and medication information. Reports can be exported as a PDF to share with your doctor or caregiver, or as a CSV for your own records. Report generation requires Premium.

Can I sync PillVault across my iPhone and iPad?+

Yes. PillVault supports iCloud sync to keep your medications, schedules, and dose history in sync across all your Apple devices. Enable it in Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → PillVault, and your data will sync automatically when you are on Wi-Fi or cellular.

Is my medication data private?+

Your medication data is stored on your devices and synced privately through Apple iCloud when enabled. PillVault does not transmit your health information to our own servers, and no account is required. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?+

Open PillVault, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, and make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Your Premium status will be restored immediately.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?+

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap [Your Name] at the top
  3. Select Subscriptions
  4. Choose PillVault: Medication Tracker
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription or change your plan

Refunds are handled by Apple in accordance with App Store policies.

Is PillVault a medical device?+

No. PillVault is a personal medication organisation and tracking tool, not a medical device, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always follow the instructions provided by your healthcare professional.

Troubleshooting

I'm not receiving dose or refill reminders

If reminders aren't appearing on your iPhone or iPad, check the following:

  1. Open Settings → Notifications → PillVault: Medication Tracker and ensure notifications are allowed
  2. Confirm reminders are toggled on for each medication inside the app
  3. Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing PillVault
  4. Make sure your device time and time zone are set correctly in Settings → General → Date & Time
  5. Restart your iPhone or iPad if reminders still don't appear

iCloud sync isn't working

  1. Confirm iCloud sync is enabled: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → PillVault: Medication Tracker — toggle it on
  2. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID
  3. Check your iCloud storage — a full account will pause sync
  4. Ensure you have a network connection on both devices
  5. Allow a few minutes for changes to propagate across devices

The app is slow or won't open

  1. Force-close the app and reopen it
  2. Restart your iPhone or iPad
  3. Update to the latest iOS/iPadOS version (Settings → General → Software Update)
  4. Ensure PillVault is updated to the latest version in the App Store

Live Activities not showing my next dose

  1. Go to Settings → PillVault: Medication Tracker and confirm Live Activities are enabled
  2. Make sure you are on iOS 16.1 or later
  3. Check that you have at least one upcoming dose scheduled for today
  4. Restart the app if the Live Activity doesn't appear
