Features

Everything you need to stay on track

Medication Organizer

Every medication. One clear view.

Organize medicines, doses, instructions, and schedules in one place - whether you take one medication or manage several prescriptions.

  • Add medications with daily, weekly, interval, or custom schedules
  • Schedule multiple doses throughout the day
  • Support for tablets, capsules, liquids, drops, sprays, patches, injections, inhalers & more
  • Add prescribing doctor details and personal notes
  • Set start and end dates for short-term courses
  • Keep complete medication history even for inactive prescriptions
Dose Reminders

Reminders built around your routine.

Set the right time for every dose and stay on schedule - never lose track of a dose again.

  • Personalised reminders for each medication and dose time
  • Custom schedules with multiple doses per day
  • See overdue doses at a glance
  • View your next dose with Live Activities on the Lock Screen
  • Use Siri & Shortcuts to log doses and check adherence hands-free
Medication Adherence

Know how you're doing.

Turn your medication history into powerful adherence insights. See patterns, build streaks, and have informed conversations with your healthcare provider.

  • Track daily, weekly, and monthly adherence
  • See completed and missed doses with trend charts
  • Build medication streaks and track your personal best
  • Per-medication adherence breakdown
  • Actionable insights to help you improve
Dose Log & History

Every dose. Every day.

Review your complete medication history and stay accountable - see exactly what was taken, when, and what was missed.

  • Log each dose with a single tap
  • Undo a mistaken log instantly
  • Clearly shows taken, on-time, and missed doses
  • Detailed timeline with scheduled vs actual times
  • 30-day history with running adherence percentage
  • Review your medication history in a calendar view
Medication Reports

Reports you can actually share.

Create detailed reports to share with your doctor, caregiver, or for your personal records - generated in seconds, directly from your dose history.

  • Comprehensive PDF report with adherence summary, history, and insights
  • Medication-by-medication performance review
  • Calendar-based adherence patterns
  • Refill status and healthcare provider information
  • CSV export for analysis or backup
  • Share with your doctor, email, or save to Files
Refill Tracker

Stay ahead of refills - never run out

Running out of an important medication should not be a surprise. PillVault tracks your supply and tells you when to refill before you hit zero.

Track your supply

Enter your current medication quantity and record refill dates as you go. PillVault automatically estimates your remaining days of supply based on your dosing schedule.

See refill status at a glance

Clear color-coded refill status indicators show how urgently each prescription needs a refill - so you can plan ahead without guessing.

Get refill reminders

PillVault sends reminders before your supply runs low, giving you enough lead time to contact your pharmacy or doctor without rushing.
