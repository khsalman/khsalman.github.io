Features

Everything you need to stay on track

From daily doses to refill planning and doctor-ready reports, PillVault keeps your medication routine organised.

💊

Medication organiser

Add tablets, capsules, liquids, drops, sprays, patches, injections, and inhalers. Set daily, weekly, interval, or custom schedules.

⏰

Dose reminders

Get a reminder for every dose time, with overdue doses shown clearly so nothing slips through the day.

📦

Refill tracker

Track your supply and see estimated days left, with colour-coded status so you can reorder before you run out.

📈

Adherence insights

See daily, weekly, and monthly adherence, build streaks, and review a per-medication breakdown with trend charts.

📅

Dose history

Log each dose with one tap, undo a mistake instantly, and review every taken and missed dose in a calendar view.

📄

Reports & export

Create a PDF report or CSV export from your dose history to share with your doctor, a caregiver, or your own records. (Premium)

🔒

Privacy-first

Your medication data is stored on your device. No account, no registration, and no medication details sent to our servers.

🌐

5 languages

Use PillVault in English, German, Spanish, French, or Portuguese.

📱

Built for Android

Designed for modern Android with Material 3 styling, supporting Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above.

How it works

From first dose to full report in minutes

Add your medications

Enter each medication, its form, dosage, and schedule. Add doctor details, notes, and start and end dates for short courses.

Log your doses

Tap to log each dose as you take it. Reminders keep you on time, and you can undo a mistaken entry at any moment.

Review & share

Check your adherence, watch your refill dates, and generate a PDF or CSV report to bring to your next appointment.
