Features

Everything for reef success

Professional-grade tools built by reef keepers, for reef keepers.

📋

Parameter tracking

Log calcium, alkalinity, magnesium, salinity, pH, temperature, and custom parameters with timestamped readings and trend charts.

💉

Dosing calculator

Intelligent calculator with a comprehensive product library. Calculate precise doses for your tank volume with stock depletion estimates.

📅

Task reminders

Never miss maintenance. Schedule reminders for water changes, equipment servicing, parameter tests, and custom tasks.

📈

Trend analytics

Visualize parameter trends over time with Apple Charts. Identify patterns and make data-driven adjustments to your reef.

Livestock tracking

Home Screen widgets

How it works

Simple workflow, serious results

Add your tank

Create a profile for each tank with volume, type, and initial parameters. Set up equipment and livestock records.

Log readings

Record water test results regularly. ReefDose calculates trends, flags concerning changes, and shows your reef health score.

Calculate & dose

Get precise dosing recommendations based on your current parameters and desired targets. Track stock levels and predict when you'll run out.
