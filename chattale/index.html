How it works

Three steps to your ChatTale

Import a conversation and ChatTale builds a full-screen deck of beautiful cards, plus a clean stats dashboard. One-on-one or group, either works.

📥

1. Import your chat

Export a WhatsApp conversation as a text file, or just drop in screenshots - ChatTale reads them with on-device text recognition. One-on-one or group, either works.

✨

2. We find the story

ChatTale analyzes your messages right on your device and surfaces the numbers and moments that make your chat yours.

▶️

3. Play & share

Swipe through your story deck, then share a card to your feed in a tap. Ready for Instagram, Stories, or the group chat itself.

Private by default

Your chats stay yours

ChatTale analyzes everything on your device. Your conversations are never uploaded to a server - not to us, not to anyone. When ChatTale saves a story, it stores only the derived results (the numbers and cards), never your raw messages. No account required to analyze a chat.
