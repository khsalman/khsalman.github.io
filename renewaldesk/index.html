Features

Your entire book, always under control

Built for independent agents who manage their own renewals — no spreadsheets, no missed expirations.

📅

Renewal pipeline

30, 60, and 90-day urgency dashboards surface the policies that need attention right now. Expired policies are flagged separately so nothing slips through.

💰

Commission forecast

12-month commission forecast charts built from your policy data. See projected income by carrier and month — free users get a 3-month view, Pro unlocks the full year.

👥

Client management

Full client profiles with linked policies, logged actions, and notes. Import directly from your iOS Contacts or add manually. Swipe to call or archive.

🔔

Smart reminders

Configurable renewal reminders at 90, 60, 30, 14, and 7 days before expiration, plus a morning digest. Never rely on memory again.

📄

Reports & export

Renewal heat map calendar, carrier donut chart, and forecast bar chart. Pro exports branded PDF and CSV reports for your records or client meetings.

📱

Widgets

Home Screen widgets show renewals due this week and the 30-day pipeline. Lock Screen circular widget shows your 30-day count at a glance. Pro only.

How it works

Simple workflow, powerful results

Add your clients & policies

Import clients from Contacts or add manually. Attach policies with premium, commission rate, carrier, and expiration date — RenewalDesk calculates expected commission automatically.

Work your pipeline

Your dashboard surfaces the 30/60/90-day urgency buckets every morning. Log actions, schedule calls, and mark policies renewed — a fresh active policy rolls forward automatically.

Report & grow

Review your commission forecast, carrier mix, and renewal heat map. Export a branded PDF summary for your own records or to share with your agency.
