Flexible scheduling

Build habits around your real life

Not every goal needs to happen every day. Vow lets you choose the schedule that actually fits your commitment - whether it's daily, weekly, monthly, or completely custom.

☀️

Daily commitments

Show up every day. Build the habit one check-in at a time.

📅

Weekly goals

Aim for a set number of days per week. Flexibility built in.

🕐

Monthly goals

Long-horizon commitments tracked across the whole month.

🆕

Yearly commitments

Annual goals with progress tracked from January to December.

🔄

Log water intake, medication, or exercise multiple times daily.

Custom frequencies

Every 2 days, 3x per week - any rhythm that works for you.

Each commitment also supports individual reminders, Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty, categories and custom categories, and optional end dates for temporary goals like a 30-day reading challenge or a Ramadan routine.

Streak protection

Build and protect your streaks

Consistency becomes easier to understand when you can see it grow. Vow shows you every angle of your progress - and gives you tools to protect it when real life gets in the way.

🔥

Streak tracking

See your current streak, longest streak, and personal best for every commitment. Streak rankings let you see which habits you've been most consistent with.

  • Current and longest streaks
  • Streak rankings across all commitments
  • Weekly progress recaps
  • Shareable streak cards

Vacation Mode

Going on holiday or taking a planned break? Vacation Mode pauses a commitment without destroying your progress. Your streak picks up right where it left off.

  • Pause any commitment penalty-free
  • Set a start and end date
  • Streak resumes automatically on return
🛡️

Streak Insurance

Life happens. Streak Insurance can help protect a valuable streak when you miss a day - so one bad day doesn't erase weeks of progress.

  • One-tap streak protection
  • Earned through consistent check-ins
  • Preserves long-run momentum
📈

See at a glance how you're doing. Success rates, total check-ins, and performance by category paint a complete picture of your consistency over time.

  • Success rates and total check-ins
  • Progress charts
  • Activity heatmaps
  • Category performance breakdown

Privacy-first by design

No account required. Your data stays with you.

No account is required to start using Vow. Your commitments and check-in history are stored locally on your device, giving you full control over your personal progress data.

  • All data stored on-device by default
  • No account, no sign-up, no email required
  • Optional iCloud sync - you choose when to enable it
  • No backend servers can read your habits
  • Works fully offline - check in anywhere
  • Delete the app to erase all data completely
