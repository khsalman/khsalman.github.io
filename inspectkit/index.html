What's new in 6.0

Everything you need for professional inspections

Photos, video, voice notes, AI polish, and a full PDF report with your branding -- from site to inbox before you drive away.

🎥

Video Findings

Attach up to 20 seconds of video to any finding. Record with the camera or pick from your library. Compressed to HEVC on-device, each clip lands in your PDF as a poster-frame tile with a play glyph and duration pill.

📷

Evidence-Stamped Photos

New photos are automatically stamped with the date, time, street address, and GPS coordinates. Library photos use their own EXIF date. Stamps appear in the PDF, too -- undeniable, on-device proof.

🧠

On-Device AI

"Improve with AI" rewrites a finding's notes into a professional observation and suggests a severity. Powered by Apple Intelligence, runs entirely on your device. Available on iPhone 15 Pro and later with Apple Intelligence enabled.

🌟

Finding Severity

Tag each flagged finding as Minor, Major, or Safety. The PDF Recommendations page sorts by severity, adds colored tags, and shows a tally -- Minor N / Major N / Safety N -- so clients see what needs attention first.

🌎

5 Languages

English, Spanish, French, Japanese, and Portuguese. Switch language at any time in Settings -- the app, all built-in templates, and the entire PDF report update instantly. Reports print in the language the app was in when you generated them.

🆕

AI Template Builder

Describe your inspection in a sentence and InspectKit generates a full checklist -- sections and items -- live in the editor. On-device AI only, no backend, no cost. Available on Apple Intelligence devices.

📸

Photos & Annotations

Capture photos directly in the app and annotate them with drawing tools. Mark issues, highlight problems, and add detailed notes -- all linked to the exact finding they document.

🎤

Voice Notes

Record findings hands-free with automatic on-device transcription. Transcription now follows the app language, so notes and PDF content stay in one language throughout.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Inspections and reports sync automatically across iPhone and iPad via your private iCloud. Video clips stay on-device only -- they never touch your iCloud storage quota.

Streamlined workflow

From site to professional report in minutes

Create

Start a new inspection from one of 19 built-in templates or a custom template you built. Add client info, property details, and up to 6 property overview photos. Reuse past clients in a tap.

Document

Work through each section. Capture annotated photos, stamp evidence with date and location, record a video, or dictate a voice note -- all linked to the exact finding. Add custom items on the fly.

Report

Tap Generate Report. InspectKit compiles everything into a polished PDF -- cover, property overview, findings, recommendations sorted by severity, photo gallery, and dual-signature sign-off -- with your branding on every page.
