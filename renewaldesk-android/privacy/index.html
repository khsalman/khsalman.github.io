Privacy Policy

RenewalDesk for Android is built on a simple principle: your client and policy data is yours. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal or business information.

Last updated: November 23, 2026  ·  Effective: November 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. All information you enter into RenewalDesk - clients, policies, carriers, renewal actions, notes, and settings - is stored on your Android device.

RenewalDesk does not require an account and never sends your data to LynxApps servers. If you choose to turn on Google Drive Backup, a copy is saved to a private app folder in your own Google Drive, which LynxApps cannot access (see section 7).

2. Contacts Access

RenewalDesk can optionally import contacts from your Android Contacts app to prefill client forms. Contact data is read locally and never transmitted to LynxApps or any other server. Clients you create this way are saved like any other client, so they are included in your Google Drive copy only if you turn on Google Drive Backup. If you deny Contacts permission, you can add clients manually at any time. Permission can be changed in Android Settings at any time.

3. Analytics & Crash Reporting

RenewalDesk uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) for anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting. Events logged include screen views, feature interactions (e.g. policy added, export triggered, paywall shown), and app stability data. No client names, policy details, premium figures, or personally identifiable information is ever included in analytics events.

Firebase data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

RenewalDesk sends local notifications for policy renewal reminders. These are scheduled on your device and never routed through a LynxApps server. You can disable notifications in Android Settings at any time, or customize reminder timing in RenewalDesk's Reminders Settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. LynxApps does not receive or store your payment information. Subscription management is handled through your Google account in Google Play.

6. Widgets

Home Screen widgets (Pro) display a snapshot of your upcoming renewals and 30-day count. This snapshot is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

7. Google Drive Backup & Sync (Optional)

Google Drive Backup is free, optional, and off until you turn it on in Settings → Google Drive Backup → Back Up & Sync. It backs up your book to your own Google Drive and keeps it the same on every Android device where you turn it on with the same Google account. After a reinstall, turning it on brings your data back.

What access the App asks for

When you turn it on, Google asks you to choose an account and approve access. RenewalDesk requests only the drive.appdata permission ("See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive"). This gives the App a hidden, private app folder in your Drive. It cannot see, open, change, or delete any of your other Drive files, and other apps cannot open its folder.

What is saved to your Drive

  • Clients: names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, notes, archived status, and client photos
  • Carriers, policies (including dates, premiums, and commission details), and renewal actions
  • Your Profile & Defaults settings: first name, default commission rate, and monthly goal
  • Records of deletions, so a record deleted on one device is also removed on your other devices

Reminder settings stay on each device and are not saved to Drive.

What the App reads from Google

  • The email address of the Google account you connect, shown in Settings and stored on your device so sync stays on the right account
  • The files in the App's own Drive folder, downloaded to your device to restore and sync your data

How the data is used and shared

Data from Google Drive is used only to back up, restore, and sync your records inside the App. It travels directly between your device and Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and never passes through LynxApps servers. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models, and we do not transfer it to anyone. No person at LynxApps reads it. Your Drive files are stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and count toward your Google storage.

RenewalDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning sync off and deleting Drive data

  • Turn off sync: Settings → Google Drive Backup → Turn Off Sync. This device stops syncing and the App's access to your Drive is removed. Your data stays on the device and in Drive.
  • Delete the Drive copy: Settings → Google Drive Backup → Delete Drive Backup. This permanently deletes the RenewalDesk copy in your Drive and turns off sync on this device. Data on the device is kept. Any other device that still has sync on uploads its data again the next time it syncs, so turn sync off on your other devices first.
  • Remove access: you can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the App's hidden data in Google Drive on the web under Settings → Manage apps.

Other than Google Drive Backup, your data is only backed up if you choose to back it up, using Android's own backup features or a file you export yourself. Those backups are handled by the service you choose, not by LynxApps.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All data on your device is deleted when you uninstall RenewalDesk, or when you clear its storage in Android Settings. This does not delete the copy in your Google Drive if you used Google Drive Backup. Use Delete Drive Backup in the App, or the Google options in section 7, to remove it. There is no account to delete on our end because we hold no data.

9. Children's Privacy

RenewalDesk is designed for professional insurance agents and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if RenewalDesk's data practices change. The latest version is always available at /renewaldesk-android/privacy/. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
