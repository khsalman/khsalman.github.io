Duplicate Photo & Video Finder ("App") is developed by LynxApps ("we," "us," "our") and uses on-device AI and machine learning to identify duplicate and visually similar photos and videos using five detection methods: dHash perceptual matching, SHA-256 exact matching, Vision ML similarity, color histogram analysis, and EXIF clustering.
