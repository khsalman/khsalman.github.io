How to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

Your iPhone can find exact duplicate photos for free with its built-in Duplicates album. If you also want to catch similar shots and duplicate videos, a dedicated cleaner can do that in a few taps. This guide covers both.

Jump to the steps

Method 1: Use the built-in Duplicates album (free)

  1. Open the Photos app and tap the Albums tab.
  2. Scroll down to Utilities and tap Duplicates. If you don't see it, your library may have no exact duplicates, or your iOS version may place it differently.
  3. Tap Select, then tap Merge on a group, or on all items you want to clean up.
  4. Confirm the merge. Photos keeps the best-quality version and combines details such as favorites and captions.

Merging is the safest option because it keeps one copy and adds the rest of the details to it. Apple's steps can change between iOS versions, so check the screen on your device if the menu looks different.

What the built-in album doesn't do: it is built for exact copies. It can miss near-identical shots, such as several photos taken a few seconds apart, and it is not a tool for sorting through similar photos quickly.

Method 2: Use a dedicated duplicate cleaner

A cleaner app can group exact copies and similar photos, then let you pick the best item in each group. Duplicate Photo & Video Finder uses several detection methods together:

  • Exact matching (SHA-256) for byte-identical copies.
  • Perceptual matching (dHash) and Vision-based similarity for resized, re-saved or near-identical photos.
  • Video duplicates, alongside photos, in one scan.

Scanning runs on your iPhone. Your photos and videos don't leave the device.

  1. Install Duplicate Photo & Video Finder from the App Store.
  2. Grant photo access and start a scan.
  3. Review each group. The app pre-selects the best item, and you can compare items side by side.
  4. Delete one group at a time, or use bulk delete with Pro. Deleted items go to Photos Trash, so you can recover them.

Tips before you delete

  • Check Recently Deleted. Items removed from Photos stay there for 30 days before they are gone for good.
  • Be careful with burst shots. Burst sequences are not duplicates. Keep the frame you want from each burst.
  • Back up first if your library matters to you. iCloud Photos or a computer backup works.

Frequently asked questions

Do deleted duplicates come back?

Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for 30 days. After that they are removed permanently.

Does the built-in Duplicates album work on every iPhone?

It is available on recent iOS versions. If you can't find it, check your version in Settings > General > About and update if you can.

Is there a free option?

Yes. The Duplicates album is free. Duplicate Photo & Video Finder is free to start, with up to 100 photo groups and 10 video groups on the free tier.
