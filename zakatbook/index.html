Zakat Calculation

Calculate your Zakat the right way

Zakat Book is designed to keep the calculation process clear while leaving asset classification decisions to you.

Gold & Silver Nisab Standards

Choose the Nisab standard that applies to your school of thought — Gold (85g) or Silver (595g). Enter today's metal price and your currency; Zakat Book calculates your Nisab threshold and 2.5% Zakat obligation instantly.

  • Choose Gold or Silver Nisab standard
  • Enter your current metal price per gram
  • Calculate your Nisab threshold automatically
  • See your exact 2.5% Zakat due
  • View a detailed calculation breakdown
  • Support for multiple currencies
  • Track Nisab threshold year over year

Every Zakatable Asset Type

Add only the categories that apply to you. No forced classifications. Zakat Book leaves asset classification decisions — especially for contested categories like property — entirely to you.

  • Cash and savings
  • Gold & silver (weight-based calculation)
  • Precious metals
  • Investments and stocks
  • Cryptocurrency
  • Business assets and inventory
  • Property (if applicable to your school)
  • Debts and liabilities (deducted)
Year-by-Year Hawl Tracking

Keep your Zakat history, not just your calculation

Your Zakat does not have to be something you remember only once a year. Over time, Zakat Book becomes your personal Zakat ledger — so you can look back at previous years instead of trying to remember what you calculated or paid.

Set up your Nisab & currency

Choose Gold or Silver Nisab during onboarding. Enter today's metal price and your currency — Zakat Book calculates your threshold and keeps a record for each Zakat year, so you can see how your Nisab threshold has changed over time.

Add your wealth & assets

Log each zakatable asset by category. For gold and silver, enter the weight and current price per gram. Debts are deducted automatically. Add only what you personally consider zakatable — Zakat Book does not impose classifications.

Record what you pay

Log each Zakat payment with date, amount, recipient, payment method, notes, and receipt photos. The app tracks your paid amount and remaining balance, updating your year status from Unpaid to Partial to Complete as you go.
