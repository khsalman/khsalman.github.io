📊

Multi-Asset Wealth Tracking

Log cash and savings, gold, silver, investments, cryptocurrency, business assets, property, and debts. Weight-based fields for metals with live price-per-gram entry. Zakat due is calculated automatically.

🪙

Gold & Silver Nisab Standards

Choose between the gold nisab (85g) or silver nisab (595g). Enter today's metal price and Zakat Book computes your nisab threshold and 2.5% obligation instantly.

🗓️

Annual Zakat Years

Create a new Zakat year for each hawl cycle. Track status — Unpaid, Partial, Complete, or Overpaid — and keep a running payment ledger with recipient and method details.

👨‍👩‍👧

Multi-Person Tracking

Manage Zakat for yourself, your spouse, and dependents from one app. Each person has their own Zakat years and assets, with a default person auto-selected when creating new years.

🌍

5 Languages with RTL Support

Full support for English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian, and Turkish. Arabic and Urdu display in right-to-left layout throughout the app, with live language switching.

📄

PDF & CSV Reports

Generate branded PDF reports of your Zakat history — useful for personal records or sharing with your family. CSV export gives you full data portability. Premium feature.

Simple Workflow

Track your Zakat in minutes

Set your nisab standard

Choose gold or silver nisab during onboarding. Enter today's metal price and your currency — Zakat Book calculates your threshold automatically.

Add your assets

Log each zakatable asset by category: cash, metals (with weight), investments, crypto, business assets, and debts as deductions. Add only what you personally consider zakatable.

Record your payments

Log each Zakat payment with date, amount, recipient, and method. The app tracks your balance and updates your year status from Unpaid to Complete automatically.
