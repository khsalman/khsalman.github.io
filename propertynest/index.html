Everything your property needs, in one organized place

A focused record-keeping app for every property you own -- not a generic notes tool.

📋

Multi-Property Catalog

Add every property you own with address, type, and key details. Each property gets its own dashboard, room list, and asset inventory. Switch between properties in a tap.

🚪

Room-by-Room Organization

Structure each property room by room. Assign appliances, fixtures, and systems to their exact location so you always know what is where -- and who installed it.

📸

OCR Document Scanning

Point your camera at any warranty card, appliance manual, or service receipt. PropertyNest extracts the text and files it under the right asset automatically.

🛠️

Maintenance and Service History

Log every repair, service visit, and self-fix with date, cost, contractor, and photos. Know exactly when the boiler was last serviced -- and what it cost.

⏰

Smart Reminders and Timeline

Set reminders for annual services, filter changes, or warranty expirations. A unified timeline shows all upcoming tasks across every property you manage.

📄

Property Passport Export

Generate a comprehensive PDF of your full property record -- perfect for insurance claims, tenant handovers, or selling. Premium feature.

Simple Workflow

Get your property organized in minutes

Add your property

Enter a property name, type, and address. PropertyNest creates a full dashboard instantly. Add as many properties as you own.

Catalog rooms and assets

Add each room, then populate it with appliances, fixtures, and systems. Capture model numbers, serial numbers, purchase dates, and warranties.

Log, scan, and remind

Scan documents with your camera, log service visits with receipts and photos, and set reminders so nothing falls through the cracks again.
