Home Maintenance

Stay ahead of the work your home needs

Keep a clear history of repairs, inspections, servicing and improvements instead of relying on memory, scattered notes or paper folders.

Create and schedule tasks

Build maintenance checklists, schedule recurring upkeep - HVAC filters, boiler service, gutter cleaning - and receive reminders before important dates so nothing gets forgotten.

Record every service visit

Log completed work with date, contractor, description, cost, and photos. Know exactly when the roof was last inspected, what it cost, and who did the work.

Track costs and history

Build a running cost record for each property. See what you've spent on maintenance, repairs and improvements - valuable context for insurance, selling or renovating.

Private by Design

Your property information is personal. It stays that way.

Property information is sensitive. PropertyNest is built with privacy and secure local storage in mind, using Apple technologies throughout - no accounts required, no data uploaded.

📱

On-Device Storage

All data - properties, rooms, assets, documents, warranties, and records - is stored locally in SwiftData on your iPhone. Nothing leaves your device without your explicit action.

🔐

No Account Required

Download and start organizing immediately. No sign-up, no email address, no account. Your property records belong to you - not to a cloud platform.

☁️

Optional iCloud Sync

Premium subscribers can enable iCloud sync via CloudKit - your data syncs to your personal Apple account, which only you can access. Sync is optional and off by default.
