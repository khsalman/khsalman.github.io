Everything you need for professional inspections

Checklists, findings, severity, and a full PDF report with your branding, all stored on your device.

📋

19 Built-in Templates

Start from a ready-made checklist for home, building, commercial, roof, HVAC, solar, rental move-in and move-out, punch list, vehicle fleet, safety audit, land and lot, and more. Build your own custom templates whenever you need them.

📷

Photo Findings

Attach photos to any finding and annotate them with notes. Each photo stays linked to the exact item it documents, so the report always matches the inspection.

🌟

Finding Severity

Tag each flagged finding as Minor, Major, or Safety. The report sorts findings by severity and shows a tally, so clients see what needs attention first.

📄

Branded PDF Reports

Generate a professional PDF with a cover page, findings, recommendations, and a sign-off page. Pro adds your own logo, company details, and signature, and removes the watermark.

🌎

5 Languages

English, Spanish, French, Japanese, and Portuguese. Switch the app language in Settings, and your templates and reports follow it.

💾

Works Offline, Stays Private

Your inspections, clients, and reports are stored on your device. There is no account to create, and inspection content is never uploaded to our servers.

Streamlined workflow

From site to professional report in minutes

Create

Start a new inspection from one of the 19 built-in templates or a custom template. Add client and property details, then reuse past clients in a tap.

Document

Work through each section, mark findings, attach photos, and set severity. Add custom items on the fly for anything the template does not cover.

Report

Generate the PDF with findings, recommendations sorted by severity, a photo section, and a sign-off page. Share it straight from your phone.
