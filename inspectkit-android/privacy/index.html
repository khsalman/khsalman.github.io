LynxApps/InspectKit Android/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your inspection data stays on your device. Inspections, findings, photos, client details, and reports are stored locally on your Android device. If you turn on Google Drive sync, a copy is kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server that receives your inspection content, and we cannot see your Drive.

1. Who We Are

LynxApps ("we," "us," "our") develops InspectKit for Android ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter is stored in the App's private storage on your device, including:

  • Inspections, checklist results, findings, and severity ratings
  • Client and property details
  • Photos attached to findings, and the location stamped on a photo when the photo stamp is on
  • Video clips and voice note text attached to findings
  • Your business profile, such as company name, logo, signature, and contact details, used only to fill in your reports
  • App settings, such as language and reminder preferences

This data is never uploaded to us. It leaves your device only if you turn on Google Drive sync (section 3) or share or export it yourself (section 7). No account, sign-in, or registration is required to use the App.

3. Google Drive Sync (Optional)

Google Drive sync is off until you turn it on in Settings or from the Dashboard. It keeps your inspections the same on every device signed in to the same Google account, and lets you restore them after reinstalling.

  • What is synced: inspections, sections, checklist items, findings, client and property details, photos and their locations, video clips, voice notes, reports, templates, and your business profile, including your logo and signature. App settings, such as language and reminders, are not synced.
  • Where it is stored: in the hidden app data folder of your own Google Drive. The files count toward your Drive storage. LynxApps has no access to them and does not receive a copy.
  • What the App can access: the App asks Google only for the drive.appdata permission. It can read and write only the files it created in its own folder. It cannot see, open, or change any other file in your Drive.
  • How it is sent: directly between your device and Google, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Files of 10 MB or more, such as video clips, wait for Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data.
  • Your Google account: the App keeps the email address of the connected account on your device so it can show it in Settings. It is not sent to us.

You can turn sync off at any time with Disconnect in Settings. This stops syncing and removes the App's access to your Drive. Your data stays on your device and in Drive until you delete it (see section 10). Use of Google Drive is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase (by Google) in release builds for two purposes:

  • Firebase Analytics: A small set of anonymous usage events, such as an inspection being created, a report being generated, or a template being used. Events never include client names, property addresses, finding notes, photos, or any other free-form content you write.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: If the App crashes, a report containing your device model, Android version, App version, and a stack trace is sent. Reports are linked to a random anonymous install identifier, not to your name or email address. No inspection content is included.

The anonymous install identifier is created the first time you open the App and is not linked to a Google account. Firebase data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

InspectKit for Android contains no advertising. We do not use any advertising SDK and do not access your advertising ID.

6. In-App Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. LynxApps never sees or stores your payment details. Google Play tells the App whether you have an active purchase so Pro features can be unlocked.

7. Exporting and Sharing

Reports are created as PDF files on your device and are shared only when you choose to share them through Android's share sheet. You can also export a backup of your inspections as a JSON file, and import it later. Backup files are created and read on your device. Nothing is sent to us during export or import.

8. Backup

The App does not use Android's automatic cloud backup or device-transfer backup. To keep a copy outside the device, turn on Google Drive sync (section 3) or export a JSON backup.

9. App Permissions

  • Internet and network state: Used for Firebase analytics and crash reports, Google Play Billing, and Google Drive sync when it is on. The network state tells sync whether you are on Wi-Fi. Inspection work does not require a connection.
  • Camera and photos: Used to capture or add photos and video clips to findings.
  • Microphone: Optional. Used for voice notes, which Android's speech recognition turns into text. Only the text is kept. Depending on your device, speech recognition may run on the device or through Google's speech service.
  • Location: Optional. Used only to stamp where a photo was taken, when the photo stamp is on. Precise or approximate location both work, and photos are still taken without it.
  • Foreground service (data sync): Used to finish large Google Drive transfers while the App is in the background, with a notification shown during the transfer.
  • Notifications: Optional. Used to send inspection reminders. Reminders are off by default, and you control them in the App and in Android Settings.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it:

  • Delete individual inspections, findings, or photos inside the App.
  • Clear the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → InspectKit → Storage → Clear storage. This erases all local data.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all local App data from your device.

If you used Google Drive sync, deleting an inspection in the App also deletes it from Drive and your other devices at the next sync. To delete the whole Drive copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps → InspectKit → Options → Delete hidden app data. Disconnecting or uninstalling the App does not delete the Drive copy.

Analytics and crash reports held by Firebase are handled under Google's retention policies. We cannot delete them for you because they are not linked to your identity.

11. Security

The App uses Android's app sandbox, which keeps its data private to the App. Google Drive sync sends data only to Google over encrypted connections, and the App's Drive folder is visible only to the App. We recommend keeping your device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Other)

  • Access: All your data is on your device and visible within the App at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete records in the App, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. Delete the Google Drive copy as described in section 10.
  • No data sale: We do not sell personal data.
  • Analytics: You can limit analytics and crash reporting through your Google account and device settings.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
