Features

Everything for reef success

Built for reef keepers who want their tank data organized, private, and always within reach.

📋

Parameter tracking

Log calcium, alkalinity, magnesium, salinity, pH, temperature, and custom parameters. Each reading is marked optimal, warning, or critical.

💉

Dosing calculator

Calculate doses for your tank volume from a built-in product library, and log every dose you make.

📊

Reef health score

One score that summarizes your tank's parameters, so you can see at a glance whether something needs attention.

🧪

Test result import

Paste or import ICP and CSV test results to add a full set of readings in one step.

📅

Task reminders

Schedule water changes, equipment servicing, and parameter tests. Reminders are rescheduled automatically after your phone restarts.

🐠

Livestock records

Keep records of fish, corals, and invertebrates with species notes and care history.

📝

Journal & expenses

Write journal entries about your tank and track equipment and running costs in one place.

📷

Home screen widget

See your tank's health from your home screen without opening the app.

🔒

Private by design

All tank data stays on your device. No accounts, no advertising, and no sign-in. The only network traffic is anonymous analytics, crash reports, and Google Play billing.

How it works

Simple workflow, serious results

Add your tank

Create a profile for each tank with its volume and type. Add your equipment and livestock records.

Log readings

Record your water test results. ReefDose flags out-of-range values and updates your tank's health score.

Calculate & dose

Calculate the dose for your tank volume, log it, and set reminders for your next water change or test.
