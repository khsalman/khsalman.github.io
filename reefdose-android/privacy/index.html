LynxApps/ReefDose Android/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your tank data stays with you. ReefDose for Android stores your water parameters, dosing records, tasks, livestock, journal entries, and expenses on your phone. If you turn on the optional Google Drive Sync, a copy is kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server that receives this information, and we cannot see it.

1. Who We Are

LynxApps ("we," "us," "our") develops ReefDose for Android ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter, including tank profiles, water test readings, dosing products and logs, scheduled tasks, livestock records, journal entries, expenses, and equipment, is stored in the App's private storage on your device. This data is never uploaded to us. No ReefDose account, sign-in, or registration is required. Your data leaves your device only if you turn on Google Drive Sync (Section 3), and then only to your own Google Drive.

ReefDose’s AI Tank Review runs entirely on your device, using Google’s on-device model (Gemini Nano). Your tank data is never sent anywhere for this. The model itself is downloaded and managed by Google Play services on your device. Optional tips and check-in reminders are local notifications: they send nothing and collect nothing.

3. Google Drive Sync (Optional)

Google Drive Sync is off by default. It is free, and it starts only when you choose "Google Drive Sync" in Settings or "Restore from Google Drive" during setup, and then approve access on Google's own permission screen. It lets you back up your tanks and keep them in step across your Android devices.

What the App accesses in your Google account

  • Permission requested: only the Google Drive "app data" permission (drive.appdata). This lets ReefDose create and read its own files in a hidden, app-specific folder in your Drive. ReefDose cannot see, open, change, or delete any of your other Google Drive files, and that folder does not appear in your normal Drive file list.
  • Account details: your Google account email address and Google's internal account identifier for your Drive. These are stored only on your device. They are used to request access for the right account and to check that every sync and every delete goes to the account you connected, so that data is never written to or removed from a different account. They are never sent to us.

What is stored in your Google Drive

  • One compressed sync file (reefdose-sync.json.gz) containing all of your ReefDose records: tanks, water test readings, tracked parameters, dosing products and dose logs, tasks, livestock, journal entries, expenses, and equipment, including any names, notes, and other text you have written.
  • Your volume unit, temperature unit, and currency settings.
  • A short list of records you have deleted, so a deletion on one device also applies on your other devices.
  • If the sync file is ever found damaged, a backup copy of it is kept in the same hidden folder before a new one is written, so nothing is lost.

Not synced: your Pro status (Google Play manages it), your active tank, reminder and theme choices, saved AI review text, and the anonymous install identifier.

How the data moves

Data travels directly between your device and Google's servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, using Google's Drive API. It never passes through any LynxApps server, and LynxApps has no access to your Google Drive or to the sync file. Syncs run in the background shortly after you make a change, when you open the App, and every few hours, only while sync is on. Data in your Google Drive is held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and counts toward your Google storage.

How we use Google user data

ReefDose uses the data it accesses through Google APIs only to provide the sync and restore feature you turned on. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models, and we do not transfer it to anyone. ReefDose's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning sync off and deleting your Drive data

  • Turn Off Sync (Settings): stops syncing. Your data stays on this device and the copy stays in your Google Drive.
  • Delete Data from Google Drive (Settings): removes all ReefDose files from your Google Drive and turns sync off on this device. Data on your device is not removed.
  • You can also remove ReefDose's hidden Drive data from Google Drive on the web (Settings → Manage apps → ReefDose → Delete hidden app data), and remove ReefDose's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If you use ReefDose on more than one device, removing access in your Google account stops syncing on all of them.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase (by Google) in release builds for two purposes:

  • Firebase Analytics: A small set of anonymous usage events: tank created, parameter logged, dose logged, task completed, livestock added, onboarding completed, paywall viewed, AI review generated, Google Drive Sync turned on, and purchase. Events include only the following details: the tank type, the parameter kind, whether a reading was in range, the task kind, the livestock category, whether you have Pro and a tank, what opened the paywall, whether an AI review succeeded and the general error type if not, where sync was turned on (Settings or setup), and for a new purchase the plan, price, and currency. Events never include water test values, tank names, product names, notes, photos, journal text, your Google account, or any other free-form content you write.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: If the App crashes, a report containing your device model, Android version, App version, and a stack trace is sent. Reports are linked to a random anonymous install identifier, not to your name or email address. No tank data is included.

The anonymous install identifier is created the first time you open the App. It is not linked to a Google account. Firebase data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

ReefDose for Android contains no advertising. We do not use any advertising SDK and do not access your advertising ID.

6. In-App Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. LynxApps never sees or stores your payment details. Google Play tells the App whether you have an active purchase so Pro features can be unlocked.

7. Exporting and Importing Data

You can export your data as CSV or PDF files. These files are created on your device, and they are shared only when you choose to share them through Android's share sheet. You can also import test results from files or pasted text that you select. Nothing is sent to us during import or export.

8. Android Backup

The App allows Android's automatic backup to include its data. If you have Google's backup turned on for your device, your ReefDose data may be included in your Google account backup, which is governed by Google's policies and your device backup settings. This is separate from Google Drive Sync: it happens even when sync is off, and "Delete Data from Google Drive" does not remove it. You can turn off device backup in Android Settings.

9. App Permissions

  • Internet: Used for Firebase analytics and crash reports, for Google Play Billing, and, only if you turn it on, for Google Drive Sync. The App works fully offline otherwise.
  • Notifications: Optional. Used to send task and parameter-test reminders. You control this in Android Settings.
  • Start on boot: Used to restore your reminders after your phone restarts.

Google Drive access is not an Android permission. It is granted through Google's own permission screen when you turn on sync, and you can remove it in your Google account (see Section 3). The App does not use your camera, photo library, contacts, or location.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device, and in your Google Drive if you use sync, until you delete it. To delete it:

  • Delete individual records inside the App. With sync on, the deletion also applies to your Google Drive copy and your other devices.
  • Use Delete Data from Google Drive in Settings to remove the Drive copy.
  • Clear the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → ReefDose → Storage → Clear storage. This erases all local data, but not your Google Drive copy.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all local App data from your device. Your Google Drive copy stays until you delete it as described in Section 3.

Analytics and crash reports held by Firebase are handled under Google's retention policies. We cannot delete them for you because they are not linked to your identity.

11. Security

The App uses Android's app sandbox, which keeps its data private to the App. Google Drive Sync uses encrypted HTTPS connections, and the sync file is stored in an app-only folder of your Google Drive protected by your Google account. We recommend keeping your device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric lock, and protecting your Google account with 2-Step Verification.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Other)

  • Access: All your data is on your device and visible within the App at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete records in the App, clear App storage, uninstall the App, or delete your Google Drive copy as described in Section 3.
  • No data sale: We do not sell personal data, including Google user data.
  • Analytics: You can limit analytics and crash reporting through your Google account and device settings.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, for example if we add new analytics events. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
