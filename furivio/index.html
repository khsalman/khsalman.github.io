Features

Everything about your pet, in one place

Build a character, enjoy its stories, and keep the practical side of pet life organised.

🎭

A character for each pet

Add a profile with name, animal type, breed, age, traits, favourite things and a photo. Furivio builds a fictional personality you can review and edit.

📖

Stories

Story Studio lets you choose the category, tone, length, setting and pets, and whether to draw on your memories. Read chapter by chapter, keep favourites, and share.

💬

Chat with the character

One conversation per pet. Replies follow the saved personality and your most relevant memories, so the character stays consistent.

📊

Comics

Turn a story into a comic of one to four panels. Edit the dialogue, pick a layout, and generate artwork for each panel. Share it as a PNG.

☀️

Daily adventures

Each day brings a thought, a mini-story, a personality moment and a suggested adventure. Add one optional daily reminder.

📅

Memories journal

Write notes about your pet, add a photo, and search later. Furivio suggests a title, category and tags, and your own note is always saved. An "on this day" prompt brings back past moments.

⏰

Care reminders

Track vet visits, medicine, grooming and other care with due dates. Repeat weekly, monthly or yearly, and mark items done to schedule the next one.

⚖️

Health log

Record weight in kilograms or pounds and add notes over time to see how your pet is changing.

📦

Export & memorial mode

Export a PDF pet profile, a memory book, or your data as JSON. When a pet has passed, Remember a pet pauses chat, daily extras and reminders until you clear it.

How it works

From profile to story in three steps

1

Create the profile

Answer a few questions about your pet. Adding a photo is optional. Furivio builds the character on your device.

2

Enjoy the character

Read stories, make comics, chat, and open a new daily adventure whenever you like.

3

Keep life organised

Care reminders, the health log and your memories stay together, so nothing important gets lost.
