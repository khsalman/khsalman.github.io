How it works

From alarm to wide awake in four steps

No willpower required at 6am - the app simply won't let you back into bed until you've moved.

⏰

1. Set a wake-up

Pick a time, an exercise or a routine, how many reps, and a repeat schedule - once, every day, weekdays, or custom days.

🔔

2. The alarm rings

At the set time UpRep sounds a real alarm using Apple's AlarmKit - it behaves like a proper alarm, even from the lock screen.

💪

3. Do the reps

Prop up your phone, get in frame, and start moving. On-device motion detection counts each valid rep live, with a big counter and progress bar.

☀️

4. Alarm dismisses

Hit your target and the alarm turns off. A short "You're up" screen celebrates the win and shows your streak.

Features

Built to actually get you moving

Camera-verified counting, chained routines, streaks, and private sync - everything runs on your device.

📷

Camera-verified reps

Real-time counting using Apple's Vision framework and on-device pose detection. You can't skip ahead - the count only moves when you do.

🔗

Routines

Chain multiple exercises into one wake-up - for example 10 squats, then 10 push-ups, then 20 jumping jacks.

📈

Difficulty & auto-progress

Pick easy, normal, or hard rep targets - or let a wake-up add a few reps each time you complete it, so you gradually get stronger.

🔥

Progress & streaks

A dedicated Progress tab tracks completed wake-ups, total reps, weekly activity, completion rate, and your current and best streaks.

✅

Honest streaks

Dismissing without finishing only counts against you once you've genuinely skipped in favor of a later wake-up - not for a single missed morning.

☁️

Private iCloud sync

Your alarms, history, and progress sync across your devices through your own private iCloud account - never our servers.

🎯

Onboarding & help

A quick intro explains the app, plus an in-app "How UpRep works" guide and per-exercise camera tips. Replay the intro anytime.

🕗

Multiple wake-ups

Schedule several alarms for different times and days - a gentle weekday routine and a tougher weekend one, side by side.

📴

Works offline

On-device first. Everything works without a connection - sync is a convenience layered on top, not a requirement.

ATT-respecting ads

All image and body-pose processing happens entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded - not to us, not to anyone. Free users see ads and anonymous crash/usage diagnostics help us improve the app; none of that ever includes your camera or motion data. Ads and tracking respect Apple's App Tracking Transparency and you can decline.
