Features

Everything an independent agent needs

Built for 1099 agents who track their own business — no spreadsheets, no guesswork.

🗂️

Deal pipeline

Kanban board with drag-and-drop stages: Lead, Under Contract, Pending, and Closed. Full deal detail with commission auto-calculation on every edit.

💰

Commission calculator

Enter sale price, gross commission rate, and agent split — ClosingDesk instantly calculates your net payout. Mark commissions received with a date.

🧾

Expense tracking

Log business expenses by category with receipt photos. Monthly breakdown charts keep your deductions organized all year long.

🚗

Mileage deduction

Track business mileage trips and calculate IRS deductions automatically using the standard mileage rate. Included in your tax summary.

📄

Tax reports

Generate branded PDF and CSV reports — tax summary, commission breakdown, expense report. Export via share sheet in seconds.

📱

Widgets & app lock

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show YTD income and goal progress. Face ID / Touch ID app lock keeps your financials private.

How it works

Simple workflow for busy agents

Add your deals

Create a deal from your pipeline. Enter the property, client, list price, sale price, commission rate, and your split — ClosingDesk does the math.

Track expenses & mileage

Log every business expense and mileage trip as they happen. Attach receipt photos so you never lose a deduction.

Export at tax time

Generate a branded PDF tax summary covering gross income, net commissions, deductible expenses, mileage, and estimated taxable income.
