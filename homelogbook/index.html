Built for Homeowners

Everything your home needs, in one place

Stop hunting through texts, photos, and sticky notes. Home Logbook keeps every detail organised and searchable.

❤️

Home Health Score

A live score computed from overdue and completed tasks shows you how well-maintained your home is at a glance — with seasonal suggestions to keep it healthy.

🏠

Room-by-Room Tracking

Organise tasks by room — kitchen, bathrooms, roof, HVAC, garden, and more. Each room shows its own task list so nothing falls through the cracks.

🔧

Maintenance Tasks

Create one-off or repeating tasks with due dates, task types, priorities, photos, and notes. Completing a repeating task auto-schedules the next one.

👷

Contractor Directory

Save plumbers, electricians, and every tradesperson you trust. Tap to call or text directly from a task — your home's trusted contacts always at hand.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Set exact reminder times or let the app default to a sensible lead-up. Warranty expiry alerts and task reminders delivered right to your lock screen.

📊

Maintenance Reports

Year-by-year reports show completed tasks, spend history, and contractor usage — useful for insurance claims, resale documentation, and planning ahead.

Simple Workflow

Up and running in minutes

Set up your home

Add your property and select your rooms in the quick onboarding flow. Home Logbook builds your home profile ready for tasks.

Log tasks & repairs

Add maintenance tasks by room, attach photos, set due dates and reminders. Repeating tasks — like gutter cleaning — reschedule themselves.

Track & report

Mark tasks complete, review your home health score, and pull up year-end reports whenever you need a maintenance record.
