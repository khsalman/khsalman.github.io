Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClosingDesk?

ClosingDesk is a commission tracker and deal pipeline app built specifically for independent real estate agents (1099). It lets you track deals from lead to close, calculate your net commission automatically, log business expenses and mileage, and generate branded PDF tax reports.

How does commission calculation work?

Enter a deal's sale price (or list price), gross commission rate, and your agent split percentage - ClosingDesk calculates your gross and net commission instantly. Example: $500,000 sale at 2.5% gross, 70/30 split = $12,500 gross, $8,750 net.

What's included in the free version?

The free version lets you track up to 3 active deals, log up to 10 expenses per month, preview reports, and use the commission calculator. Unlimited deals, unlimited expenses, PDF/CSV export, and widgets require a Pro subscription.

How much does Pro cost?

Pro is sold through Google Play, which shows the current plans and prices before you buy.

Does ClosingDesk sync across my devices?

No. ClosingDesk for Android keeps your data on the device you use it on. To move your records to another device, create a CSV data backup and restore it there.

What data does ClosingDesk collect?

Your deal data, commissions, expenses, and photos stay on your device. ClosingDesk uses Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous crash reports only - no financial data is ever included. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I export my data?

Pro users can export a branded PDF tax summary, commission report, expense report, and a full CSV data backup from the Reports tab.

How do I track mileage?

Add mileage trips in the Expenses tab. ClosingDesk calculates your IRS-standard mileage deduction and includes it in your annual tax summary report.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ClosingDesk → Cancel subscription. Your Pro access continues until the end of the billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open ClosingDesk → tap the Pro upgrade button → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to Google Play with the same Google account used for the original purchase.

Is my data safe if I lose my phone or change devices?

ClosingDesk doesn't sync to the cloud. Create a CSV data backup regularly and keep a copy somewhere safe, so you can restore your records on a new device.

What's the App Lock feature?

App Lock lets you require your fingerprint, face unlock, or your device screen lock to open ClosingDesk. Enable it in Settings. The app locks automatically when it goes to the background.
