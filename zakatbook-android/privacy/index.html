LynxApps/Zakat Book Android/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your financial data never reaches us. Zakat Book for Android is local-first — all asset data, Zakat calculations, and payment records are stored on your device. We have no servers and never collect, receive, or store any of your financial information.

1. Who We Are

LynxApps ("we," "us," "our") develops Zakat Book for Android ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use the App and how it is handled.

2. Financial & Personal Data

All data you enter — asset values, gold and silver holdings, cash amounts, investments, debts, Zakat payment records, the names of people and recipients you add, your notes, and any photos you attach — is stored on your device. This data is never sent to LynxApps or any third party. No account or registration is required to use the App.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase (by Google) for two purposes in release builds:

  • Firebase Analytics: Usage events such as "Zakat year created", "payment recorded", "report exported", and which screens are opened, together with your device model, Android version, app version, language and currency code. Firebase links these events to a random app-instance ID (and may read the advertising ID, see section 4), not to your name or Google account. No financial amounts, asset values, names, notes, photos, or other personal data are ever included in analytics events. Analytics run only in release builds, not in debug builds.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: If the App crashes, a report containing device model, Android version, app version, and a stack trace is sent. No financial data or personal information is included in crash reports.

We use this data only to understand how the App is used and to fix problems. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. Google keeps it according to Firebase's data retention rules.

Firebase data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

The App currently shows no ads, and no ad network receives any data from it.

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads software so that ads can be offered to free users in a future version, and it has Android's advertising ID permission. Firebase Analytics (section 3) may read your device's advertising ID for aggregated usage statistics. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

If ads are introduced, this policy will be updated first. Free users would then see ads from Google Mobile Ads, which may use the advertising ID and device information to choose ads; a consent prompt would let you choose how your data is used for ads, and Premium subscribers would see no ads. Ad data would be governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Live Metal Prices and Exchange Rates

The App fetches real-time gold and silver spot prices and currency exchange rates (used to convert totals between currencies) from third-party market data providers. These requests contain no personal or financial data — only a request for the current prices and rates. As with any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address. Your asset quantities and amounts are never sent externally.

6. In-App Purchases

All Premium subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. LynxApps never sees or stores your payment details. Purchase validation happens through Google's secure billing infrastructure.

7. App Permissions

  • Photos: Optional. The App asks for no camera or storage permission. When you attach a receipt to a payment or a picture to a person, you choose the image with Android's photo picker, and the App can read only the image you pick. Photos are stored on your device.
  • Internet: Required to fetch live gold & silver prices and exchange rates, for analytics and crash reports, and for Google Play purchases. Your financial data is never sent to us.
  • Notifications: Optional. Used for Hawl reminders (before your Zakat is due, on the day, and while a year is still unpaid) and, if you keep them on, a check-in before Ramadan and a weekly summary. Reminders are created on your device. You control them in the App's notification settings and in Android Settings.
  • Biometric lock: Optional. If you turn on the app lock, Android's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock prompt protects the App. The App only receives a yes or no result; it never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your Zakat data is stored on your device. It is never stored on our servers. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual years, payments, people or recipients in the App at any time.
  • Uninstall the App to permanently remove all App data from your device.
  • Email lynxapps.contact@gmail.com for any other request, including deletion of analytics or crash data linked to your device. We will reply within 2-3 business days.

9. Security

The App relies on Android's built-in security model. App data is stored in the app's private sandbox, protected by Android's file-level encryption on supported devices. Your financial data is never sent to us.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Other)

  • Access: All your data is on your device and visible within the App at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete records in the App or uninstall it. For any other deletion request, email us (section 13).
  • No data sale: We do not sell personal data.
  • Advertising ID: Reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Analytics: Analytics and crash data are processed by Firebase and are not linked to your name. Resetting your advertising ID breaks its link to past analytics, and you can email us to ask for deletion of data linked to your device.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us:

  • Email: lynxapps.contact@gmail.com
  • Response time: within 2-3 business days

Privacy by design. Your Zakat records are yours alone. Zakat Book processes everything on your device — your wealth data never reaches us,.
