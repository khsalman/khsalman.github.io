Features

Everything your Zakat calculation needs

From live metal prices to family-wide tracking, Zakat Book handles the complexity so you can give with confidence.

👪

Multi-person tracking

Manage Zakat for yourself and your entire family from a single app. Each person gets their own asset profile and Hawl.

🌹

Full asset management

Add gold, silver, cash, investments, business assets, and receivables. Debts are automatically deducted from your zakatable wealth.

📈

Live metal prices

Real-time gold & silver spot prices power automatic Nisab calculation. Always know whether you've crossed the threshold.

⚖

Nisab standards

Choose Gold (85g) or Silver (595g) Nisab standard. The threshold updates live with current market prices.

📅

Hawl tracking

The app tracks your Islamic lunar year and sends timely reminders before your Hawl ends so you never miss your obligation.

💰

Payment recording

Log every Zakat payment with recipient, amount, date, and an optional receipt photo for a complete giving history.

🔒

Privacy-first

All your financial data stays on your device. No account, no registration, no cloud sync — your wealth is your business.

🌐

5 languages

Full UI in English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian, and Turkish with complete RTL support for Arabic and Urdu.

📄

PDF reports & export

Generate full Zakat year reports and export to spreadsheet for your records or your accountant. (Pro)

How it works

From assets to obligation in minutes

Add your assets

Enter gold, silver, cash, investments, and business assets. Debts are deducted automatically to give you your net zakatable wealth.

Check your Nisab

Live metal prices update the Nisab threshold automatically. The app tells you clearly whether Zakat is due this year.

Record & report

Log payments as you give, track your Hawl, and generate a full PDF report for the year — all without leaving the app.
