How to digitize receipts on iPhone

Paper receipts fade, get lost and are hard to total at tax time. With an OCR receipt scanner on your iPhone, you can capture each one in seconds, keep the details in one place and export them when you need them.

Jump to the steps

Step 1: Scan or import the receipt

Open the camera in the app and point it at the receipt, or import an existing receipt photo from your photo library. Keep the receipt flat and well lit, with the full text in the frame.

Step 2: Let OCR read the details

Optical character recognition (OCR) reads the merchant, date and amount from the image. Receipt Digitizer runs this on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework, so the scan works without an internet connection.

Step 3: Review and confirm

Check the extracted details against the paper receipt. If OCR missed something or read a number wrong, edit it in a tap, then save. Taking a few seconds to check now saves time later.

Step 4: Let it categorize

Each expense is added to your history and sorted into a category. Use the built-in categories, or create your own to match how you spend, such as groceries, travel, or client work.

Step 5: Track budgets and spending

Set a budget for a category or for your total spending, then watch the progress as you go. Spending dashboards show category breakdowns and monthly trends, so you can see where your money goes.

Step 6: Export when you need the records

  • PDF: a polished expense report to share with an employer, an accountant or for your own records. Useful for reimbursement claims.
  • CSV: raw expense data for spreadsheets, accounting software or your own analysis.

Tips for better results

  • Scan soon after you buy. Thermal receipts fade over time, and a scan keeps the details even after the paper is gone.
  • Check the amount first. The total is the field most worth confirming before you save.
  • Keep the original for tax or reimbursement claims if your local rules require it. Check what your tax authority or employer accepts.
  • Use manual entry for expenses without a receipt. You can add them with the same level of detail.
